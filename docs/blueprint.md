# **App Name**: VibeFlow

## Core Features:

- User Onboarding & Profile: Allow users to register and log in via Email/Password or Google Sign-In, and manage their basic user profile.
- Mood & Preference Selector: Interactive user interface for selecting a mood archetype (e.g., Adventure, Calm), desired media format (Book, Series, Movie), and reality preference (Fiction vs. Based on a Real Story).
- Narrative Context Input: A dedicated text area for users to provide a brief personal narrative or describe their desired emotional outcome, acting as a nuanced input for recommendations.
- AI-Powered Media Recommendations: An AI tool that processes the user's selected mood, preferences, and narrative to generate and present a curated list of personalized media suggestions.
- Recommendation Display & Discovery: Present a curated list of media items with their cover images and titles, enabling users to visually browse and discover new content aligned with their inputs.
- Detailed Media View & 'Why': Display comprehensive information for each recommended media item, including title, author/creator, cover image, synopsis, and an AI-generated explanation of why this specific media matches the user's inputs.
- Community Contributions & Feedback: Enable users to submit and view reviews, star ratings, and community feedback for individual media items.

## Style Guidelines:

- Dark color scheme, evoking a sense of introspection and focus, while highlighting content effectively.
- Primary interactive color: HSL(270, 60%, 70%) or RGB #BE8FF2, a soft yet vibrant violet to signify personalization and subtle guidance without being overly assertive.
- Background color: HSL(270, 15%, 15%) or RGB #2A2330, a deep, desaturated indigo-purple providing a sophisticated and calming backdrop that contrasts well with interactive elements.
- Accent color: HSL(240, 70%, 55%) or RGB #434BDB, a rich blue-purple that is analogous to the primary color, providing distinct visual separation and emphasis for call-to-action elements.
- Headlines and prominent text: 'Belleza', a humanist sans-serif, to convey a modern, artistic, and design-aligned aesthetic, suitable for impactful titles.
- Body text and long-form content: 'Alegreya', a humanist serif, to provide an elegant, intellectual, and comfortable reading experience for synopses and reviews, suitable for literary content.
- Use clean, minimalist outline icons for clear visual communication, representing moods, media types, and user actions without cluttering the interface.
- Adopt a clean, spacious, card-based layout for media recommendations, featuring ample negative space to direct user focus and enhance content visibility across various screen sizes.
- Implement subtle and purposeful animations for transitions between screens and interactive elements, ensuring a smooth and responsive user experience without distracting from content.
